We're so excited to welcome you to the new Open Farms WNY website! Whether you're a longtime volunteer, a first-time visitor, or someone who just stumbled across us — we're glad you're here.

## What We're About

Open Farms WNY is a nonprofit working farm in Western New York. We grow vegetables, care for animals, run educational programs for school groups, and offer a therapeutic farming program for individuals who benefit from purposeful outdoor work.

Everything we do centers on community. The farm isn't just a place to grow food — it's a place to grow relationships, resilience, and belonging.

## Come Visit

We're open for volunteers every Saturday morning (8 AM – 12 PM) year-round, and Thursday evenings (5 PM – 8 PM) from spring through fall. No experience needed — just show up.

Once a month on the first Sunday, we host Family Farm Day — an open event for the whole community, complete with animal encounters, activities for kids, and fresh produce.

## How You Can Help

The farm runs on volunteer labor and community generosity. Here's how to get involved:

- **Volunteer** — come work a session (it's free and you'll love it)
- **Donate** — even small gifts directly support our animals, programs, and operations
- **Spread the word** — share our website with anyone who might benefit from a farm connection

We're so grateful for every person who shows up, chips in, and believes in what we're building here. See you at the farm!
